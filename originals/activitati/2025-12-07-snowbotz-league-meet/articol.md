Opt echipe au participat la întâlnirea ligii: Clockworks #19075, Sparktech #24345, ArtRobotiX #19059, Renaissance Robotics #22226, MasterMinds #21050, HYPERCUBE #20925, Robo-Sapiens #19117, Zenith #19084.
